Pasta in white sauce

Ingredients

  • Two tablespoons of butter
  • Three tablespoons of all-purpose flour
  • One medium cup of milk
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Nutmeg (optional)

Instructions

  1. Wondering how to make easy white sauce for pasta? The first thing you should do is put two tablespoons of butter in a saucepan over medium heat to melt. It's essential not to use very high temperatures, as the goal is for it to melt slowly, not burn.

  2. While the butter is melting, you can take a small saucepan and add the milk you prepared earlier in a medium-sized cup. Remember to keep the saucepan over low heat, as you'll need to remove it just before the milk starts to boil.

  3. Once the butter has melted, add the flour to the saucepan. It's best to add it gradually so it blends well with the butter and no lumps form. Remember to stir the mixture constantly; this will create a completely smooth paste with a creamy texture. Once you've added all the flour, stir for a minute to ensure the mixture is perfectly combined.

  4. Once you have this creamy paste, pour in the milk. Just like with the flour, it's essential to add the milk to the mixture gradually. The key is to create a smooth, creamy, and lump-free texture. Stir the mixture with a spoon throughout the process, until everything is well combined and it begins to boil and thicken. The heat should remain low; otherwise, the ingredients won't blend properly, lumps will form, and it could even burn.

  5. After pouring in the milk, you'll see the mixture begin to boil. Continue stirring well until it thickens, and when it does, take about two minutes to remove it from the heat, but always keep an eye on it so it doesn't thicken too much.

  6. Once the sauce has thickened, remove the pan from the heat and add salt and pepper. The amount will depend on your personal taste and the level of flavor you want in the recipe. If you like nutmeg, now is the time to add the desired amount.

You've now finished making the homemade white sauce for pasta, but if you want to add any other ingredients, that's up to you. Now all that's left is to add the sauce to your chosen pasta and wait for your guests to lick their fingers.
